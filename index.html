Welcome to Universal OS

What is Universal Existence?

It's a 2D platformer containing plenty of mechanics that'll freshen up every level (and soon enough, funny voice lines). You can go from firing bullets at enemies to raving at a deadly disco party and even fighting for your life in a full-on tower defense! So much to do in a platforming course...

Don't worry, each mechanic will also be expanded upon so you get to see the expansion of your favorite mechanics!

The game's demo is currently in V1, with the first area of the game: Natural Plains, with all levels/difficulties and extra speedrun options in settings (press Esc on keyboard or the Menu button on controller).
